<!--
Not only is this a relatively data-packed page, it’s also passed through different owners over the years. Codecademy as a company has been growing a lot; any company changing shape or size will need to reorganize its internal teams regularly.
Cultures and priorities of teams can drastically influence the direction a page takes. Codecademy in 2019 didn’t have enough engineering resources to devote much attention to the catalog: so it didn’t change much that year.
2020’s “DiscoNav” team, whose logo is a dolphin named “Adolpho” for reasons I still do not understand, was more of a fun, experimental team dedicated to the discovery and navigation on the site. So the catalog got a lot of design explorations and a radical new look.
2021’s “Reach” team isn’t as focused on drastically redoing the catalog so much as incrementally improving it to appeal more to first timers: hence the splashy colors and visually distinct design.
This is all to say: the people and structures behind a product have a real impact on how that product evolves.
